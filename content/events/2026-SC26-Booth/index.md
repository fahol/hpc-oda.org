Meet the ODA Team members and chat about Operational Data Analytics. Whether you're a newcomer to ODA or an experienced user, we would love to hear about your interest, experiences, use-cases, lessons learned.

This is shortly after the BoF "Operational Data Analytics: Agentic AI for HPC Facilities". So if you have still topics left to discuss, come and join us!

The ODA community's peer-reviewed workshop, [HPC-ODA 2026](/workshop2026/), follows on Friday, November 20, 8:30am to 12:00pm CST, in Room W190b.
