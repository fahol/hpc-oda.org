The program for the **1st International Workshop on HPC Operational Data Analytics (HPC-ODA 2026)** is final, and submissions are closed.

The workshop is Friday, 20 November 2026, 8:30am to 12:00pm US Central, in Room W190b at McCormick Place in Chicago. The agenda and the accepted presentations are on the [workshop page](/workshop2026/). The [SC26 schedule entry](https://sc26.conference-program.com/session/?sess=sess153) is the source for the times, the room, and the author list. That schedule does not label talks as full papers or short papers. The paper deadline was extended to August 12, 2026, and that deadline has passed.

The community also has a Birds of a Feather session, **Operational Data Analytics: Agentic AI for HPC Facilities**, on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, 12:15pm to 1:15pm US Central, in Room W183b. Four panelists will give short statements, followed by open discussion with Mentimeter polls. The official schedule lists the session leader and additional session leaders. It does not name which four people are the panelists. Details are on the [BoF page](/events/2026-sc26-oda-bof/).
