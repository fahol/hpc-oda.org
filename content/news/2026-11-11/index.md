The ODA community has two sessions at SC26 in Chicago.

**Birds of a Feather: Operational Data Analytics: Agentic AI for HPC Facilities**<br>
Wednesday, November 18, 12:15pm to 1:15pm CST, Room W183b<br>
Panelists Keiji Yamamoto (RIKEN R-CCS), Steven Farrell (NERSC), Avisek Naug (HPE), and Kevin Menear (NLR) open a discussion, moderated by Dong Dai (University of Delaware), on where AI agents fit in HPC facility operations. Bring your questions and your phone for the Mentimeter polls. [Session details](/events/2026-sc26-oda-bof/)

**HPC-ODA 2026: 1st International Workshop on HPC Operational Data Analytics**<br>
Friday, November 20, 8:30am to 12:00pm CST, Room W190b<br>
Seven full papers, three short papers, and one lightning talk, selected from 36 submissions. [Full program](/workshop2026/#program)
